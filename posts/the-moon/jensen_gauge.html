x₁=4 and x₂=9 stand in for m²f₁f₂ and (m+1)²f₁f₂ — two consecutive points on one ray of Chapter 1's grid. The alternating sign trick means one of them arrives weighted by cos²θ, the other by sin²θ — here abstracted to a single free weight λ. The gap between the amber and teal dots never goes negative, for any λ or any u: that's Jensen's inequality doing real work, on every ray, everywhere in the grid.
