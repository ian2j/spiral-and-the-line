    Each point is a running total of n−s for n = 1…M, connected in order — the path winds inward toward a fixed center as more terms are added. The selected heights are real, known zeros of the Riemann zeta function. At σ = 0.5 (the critical line), the spiral closes in near the origin: a zero. Drag the fader off σ = 0.5 and the same spiral visibly misses. Path length is extended a few multiples past M purely to make the winding legible; the diamond marks where the truncation f(s) is actually evaluated.
