    ηM(s) is computed directly from the real partial sum of η up to M = ⌊t/π⌋ terms. T(s) is the closed-form Fresnel estimate from Chapter 3's stationary-phase machinery. Drawn tip to tail: if they were exact opposites, the second arrow would land back on the origin. On the critical line, at a real zero, it very nearly does — and |T(s)| sits at almost exactly 1/√2, the value the formula predicts for σ=1/2 regardless of height.
