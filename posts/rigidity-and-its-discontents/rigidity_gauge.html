The curve is S(u)/|F(½,t)|² − 1, the normalized energy above baseline. Where it's positive (teal), the theorem guarantees F(σ,t) ≠ 0. That region only exists at all when the curvature ratio is above 1 — below it, the quartic remainder overwhelms the quadratic gain everywhere, and the argument gives no zero-free zone. c₀ and C are both set to 1 here for legibility; only the shape and the threshold crossing are the point.
